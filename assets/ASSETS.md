# Profile artwork

Four coordinated illustrations were created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool for this profile. Art direction: cream storybook forest cottage, warm sage green and honey ochre, painted gouache color blocks. The selected “奶油童话工作室” concept preview was used as a style reference.

These are illustrative project covers, not screenshots or evidence of hardware implementation. CommScope Lab is a browser-based communication simulation; the satellite and dish are visual motifs.

The generated artwork was resized proportionally and encoded as WebP for page loading. No third-party image hosts, analytics, dynamic badge services or runtime scripts are used by this README.

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `forest-studio-banner.webp` | Wide forest-cottage banner, rabbit painting with headphones |
| `commscope-lab.webp` | Woodland signal laboratory with satellite, dish and waveform |
| `resonance-studio.webp` | Cottage music corner with headphones and musical light |
| `palette-atelier.webp` | Forest art nook with landscape, palette and six color swatches |

## Generation brief

Shared: finished raster illustration assets, not website mockups; tactile storybook gouache, cream paper, moss and sage green, honey ochre and muted peach; clear painted shapes and warm window light. No browser UI, buttons, badges or watermarks.

- Banner: 3:1 horizontal forest-cottage studio; white rabbit wearing brown headphones paints beside a vintage radio, books, coffee mug and white flowers; leafy arched windows and golden afternoon light; no text.
- CommScope Lab: 3:2 woodland signal laboratory; cream satellite dish, sage oscilloscope with an illustrative waveform and constellation dots, satellite and globe, leaves and white flowers; title “CommScope Lab”.
- Resonance Studio: 3:2 cottage music corner; brown headphones, sage music display, amber and peach sound bars and light ribbon, timber window, ivy and flowers; title “Resonance Studio”.
- Palette Atelier: 3:2 cottage art nook; reference landscape painting, six sage/moss/yellow/peach swatches, wooden palette, paint brushes, foliage and flowers; title “Palette Atelier”.

## Editing the profile

The root `README.md` appears automatically on the Overview tab because this public repository shares its owner's username. Keep the repository named `Mecony` and keep `README.md` in its root. GitHub Pages is not required.

Edit the `href` for destinations and the `src` for image locations separately. Project covers lead to repositories; “Demo” leads to the published tool and “Code” leads to source. Project implementations remain in their own repositories.
